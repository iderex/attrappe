# NNNN. One sentence naming the decision

## Context

What was true before, and what forced a choice.

## Options considered

The options, with at least one that was genuinely available.

## Decision

The decision, in one sentence in the present tense, followed by whatever detail
the sentence needs to be unambiguous.

## Reasons

One paragraph per reason, each one something a reader could disagree with.

## Costs accepted

What this decision gives up, and what it will cost later. This is the section
everybody skips and the one that tells a future reader whether the decision was
made with open eyes.

## Consequences

What is now fixed for everything downstream.

## Status

One of: proposed, accepted, superseded by NNNN.
